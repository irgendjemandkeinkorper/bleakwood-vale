BLACKWOOD VALE

a surreal gothic mystery, told together, one candle at a time
❦ ✦ ❦

A death has come to the fog-bound town of Blackwood Vale. Around this table — or this single guttering screen — you will unravel what happened, layer the tale with omens and confessions, and write the whole dreadful chronicle down for posterity.

A gothic re-imagining after the design of Tall Pines by Miles Gaborit.
All card text herein is original. For 1–6 storytellers, about half an hour each.
A word before the dark: this game begins with a death and invites worse. Agree as a group that anyone may strike any element from the story at any time — no questions asked, no reasons owed. The stricken thing simply never was.

Each incident colours the questions asked, the scenes dealt, and the shape of the truth.
